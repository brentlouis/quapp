Trust & safety
Trust & safety rules (Sep 28)


Organizers
One account type. Anyone managing queues can ask to be verified from Profile: organization, type, position, the office's public number. No ID photos.
Admin (you) calls that number. Approve → badge on every queue they post.
Unverified: may post, no badge, “Unverified organizer” note on Queue detail, 1 live queue at a time, not in “Open now across Bohol”.
Badge promise: Quapp confirmed who runs the account. It does not vouch for each queue's details.


Queuers
No ID checks. Max 2 accounts per device (install ID), 1 active ticket per time slot.
Organizers can remove a ticket: prank (counts as no-show, goes to admin), duplicate, asked to leave.
Anyone can report a queue; reporters stay anonymous.


Accounts
Suspended users are blocked at login and see the reason. Their live queues close (QUEUE_CLOSED, never a no-show) and their tickets are released.
Revoke = badge removed, queues keep running. Suspend = account blocked.


User behaviour: people usually know which queue they want from the organizer's own announcement. The design leans on that: the unverified note tells them to check it, and Share link / QR is the main way in.
Admin (web)
Data model for trust & safety


User: phone (unique), phone_verified (false until SMS), password_hash, device_install_id, status ACTIVE | SUSPENDED, suspended_reason, suspended_at.


Organizer fields on User: org_name, org_type, verification_status NONE | PENDING | VERIFIED | REJECTED | REVOKED, verified_at, verified_by, admin_note.


VerificationRequest: user_id, org_name, org_type, position, office_phone, status, submitted_at, reviewed_at, reviewed_by. Answers deleted after a decision; the result and note stay.


Report: queue_id, reporter_id (never shown to the organizer), reason, note, status OPEN | DISMISSED | ACTIONED, created_at.


TicketRemoval: ticket_id, by_organizer_id, reason PRANK | DUPLICATE | ASKED_TO_LEAVE. PRANK adds a no-show.


Queue: no badge field. The app reads the organizer's verification_status, so a revoke shows everywhere at once.


Admin is a small web page on the FastAPI server, not an Android screen.
