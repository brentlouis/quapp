Use case: ui-mockup.
Create a polished high-fidelity visual concept board for Quapp, an existing Android public community queue management app in Bohol, Philippines. New experimental direction: Swiss modernist civic interface. Landscape board, three equal-size flat Android screen artboards side by side, front-on without perspective, each full screen visible and large readable UI. Small board heading "QUAPP / MODERNIST STUDY" and screen captions "01 / Browse", "02 / My ticket", "03 / Live console". Pale neutral background, ample balanced margins, focus entirely on interface.

Design system: white #FFFFFF surfaces, cool near-white #F5F6F8 ground, near-black #171C26 text, secondary #596273, cobalt #2447D8 primary and pale blue #EEF2FF. Clean IBM Plex Sans-like grotesk sans serif typography, large bold sans numerals, monospaced small counts. Swiss asymmetric editorial hierarchy with strict alignment, strong whitespace and precise thin gray dividers. Restrained 8px corner radii, crisp flat rectangular controls. Cobalt rectangular primary buttons, simple outline icons. Green status labels with words. No gradients, texture, tickets cutouts, perforations, barcode, torn paper, serif type, glass effects, photos, decorative charts, shadows, or 3D phone hardware. Minimal status bars and gesture bars.
